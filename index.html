Position

The method is the deliverable

Four trading strategies I built turned out not to work, and I reported that rather than shipping them. The main thing my thesis set out to prove did not hold, and the thesis says so. What I am offering is the discipline underneath that: write down what would count as success before you look at the answer, so you cannot move the goalposts afterwards.

That discipline is also how I work with AI tools: written rules about what the tool may decide on its own, a requirement that every claim carries its source, and limits enforced in code rather than asked for politely. How I work →
